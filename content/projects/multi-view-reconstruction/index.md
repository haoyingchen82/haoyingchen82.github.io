## Overview

This project is a practical exploration of classical multi-view geometry and 3D reconstruction. Through independent implementation, I am working to understand how camera models and geometric relationships connect multiple images to three-dimensional structure.

## Scope

The exploration follows the main concepts of a classical multi-view reconstruction pipeline:

- **Camera calibration:** understanding camera intrinsics and the mapping between 3D points and image observations.
- **Image correspondence and epipolar geometry:** studying how matching observations constrain the relationship between views.
- **Fundamental and essential matrices:** connecting image correspondences with relative camera geometry.
- **Relative camera pose and triangulation:** exploring how camera motion and 3D points can be recovered from multiple views.
- **Structure from motion:** bringing these ideas together within a classical reconstruction pipeline.

## Learning Focus

The emphasis is on multi-view geometry foundations and reconstruction implementation, rather than a novel method or a formal research contribution. This work supports my broader interest in how visual observations provide information about 3D space.
